# Power BI project guide

Use [README.md](README.md) for the existing semantic model, source-data export, opening the PBIP and refreshing local tables. No sign-in is required.

The six report-page definitions are experimental source: executive, screening, payment risk, model performance, investigation and statistics. They are retained for future authoring; rendering, slicer interactions and displayed totals have not been validated. Do not present them as finished dashboards.

[DAX measures](DAX_MEASURES.md) and [theme](FinCrimeAI_Theme.json) are available for that future work. The project builder can regenerate source, but will overwrite edits and is not needed for a normal refresh.
